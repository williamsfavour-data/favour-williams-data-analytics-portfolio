# Sales Performance Analysis & Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes synthetic sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and monthly trends.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into a clear dashboard that could support business decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focused on:

- How much revenue and profit were generated?
- Which products performed best?
- Which regions generated the most revenue?
- How did sales change over time?
- What was the overall profit margin?
- Which areas could the business improve?

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Data analysis
- KPI development
- Dashboard creation
- Data visualization
- Business insights
- Profitability analysis

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Total Revenue:** ₦95.37M
- **Total Profit:** ₦27.24M
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 28.6%
- **Top Product:** Laptop
- **Top Region:** Abuja

The analysis also compared product and regional performance and examined monthly revenue trends.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business could:

1. Continue monitoring the performance of high-revenue products.
2. Investigate factors contributing to strong regional performance.
3. Monitor monthly sales trends to identify periods of growth or decline.
4. Track profit margins alongside revenue when evaluating performance.
5. Use the dashboard for regular management reporting.

## 📊 Deliverables

- Excel sales analysis workbook
- Interactive-style KPI dashboard
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Business insights and recommendations
