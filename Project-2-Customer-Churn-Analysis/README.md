Project 2: Customer Churn Analysis

Tool: Microsoft Excel
Project Type: Sample Business Analysis
Data: Synthetic customer data

Project Overview

This project explores customer churn patterns to understand how customer characteristics and subscription choices may relate to customer retention.

The analysis uses synthetic customer data created for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not represent a real client or company.

Objectives

- Calculate the customer churn rate.
- Compare churn across contract types and subscription plans.
- Examine churn patterns across regions and customer tenure.
- Estimate the revenue associated with customers who have churned.
- Provide practical recommendations to support customer retention.

Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Customer segmentation
- KPI analysis
- Data visualization and dashboard development
- Business insights and reporting

Key Findings

The workbook reports 150 customers, including 43 churned customers, giving a churn rate of approximately 28.7%. The estimated revenue associated with churned customers is ₦9,919,700.

These findings are based on the synthetic dataset and should not be interpreted as actual company performance.

Recommendations

- Review churn patterns across contract types and subscription plans.
- Investigate customer groups with comparatively high churn.
- Consider targeted retention communication for customers at risk of leaving.
- Monitor churn rate and revenue at risk over time to evaluate retention efforts.
